# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
General curious public visiting a personal portfolio — leisurely browsing, discovering the work and world of a curator, historian, and craftsman. No commercial intent. This is an invitation into someone's intellectual and creative life, not a conversion funnel.

### Brand Personality
**Three words:** Eclectic, handmade, intellectual.

The site should feel like a carefully layered study or workshop — personal, full of quiet depth, with visible signs of a thinking, making person. Not polished toward commerce. Not aggressively reduced.

### Aesthetic Direction
Cinematic and editorial with warmth and tactile character. Think: early 20th century printed matter, hand-set type, natural materials, artisanal craft. References from the waldorf/granola tradition — organic, unhurried, slightly irregular. Things that look like they were made by a person, not assembled from a UI kit.

**Light mode** (warm cream + deep charcoal + terracotta). The browsing context is leisurely, thoughtful, probably daytime.

**Anti-references:**
- Not commercial/SaaS-polished: [loom.com](https://www.loom.com/) — too smooth, too optimized, too startup
- Not grid-as-statement minimalism: [grids.obys.agency](https://grids.obys.agency/) — too art-directed for its own sake, cold

### Typography Direction
Strong preference for serif or artisanal display fonts. Look toward:
- Early 20th century American/European type design (Goudy era, old-style humanist serifs)
- Fonts with visible craft character — slight irregularity, historical resonance, warm stroke variation
- Waldorf/granola aesthetic — organic, a little worn, rooted in tradition

**Avoid:** The modern "editorial" reflex — Playfair Display, Cormorant, DM Sans are in use but are overused patterns. Open to replacing them with something less expected. Whatever lands should feel like it was chosen, not defaulted to.

### Design Principles

1. **Intellectual intimacy over commercial polish** — the interface is an invitation, not a pitch. Trust the visitor to linger.
2. **Eclectic layering over rigid systems** — asymmetry and unexpected juxtapositions are features. The site can surprise.
3. **Tactile warmth** — weights, textures, and typographic details that read as handmade and considered.
4. **Unhurried pacing** — generous whitespace and quiet reveals. Not optimized for anything.
5. **Content-first humility** — the work speaks; the interface whispers.
